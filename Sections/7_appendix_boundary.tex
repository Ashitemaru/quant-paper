\section{Information Boundary and Qualification Rules}
\label{app:boundary}

\subsection{Authorization and Final-Assessment Boundary}

The research harness is provisioned with its public suite, authorized tooling, research feedback, and remaining-resource state. Quantitative strategy execution operates under an independently scoped market-data capability held exclusively by the trusted supervisor, which mediates all requests against permitted versions, dates, instruments, fields, and data availability limits. Participant programs cannot broaden this scope by supplying alternative dates or forwarding internal authorization headers. Final evaluation is conducted using the registered sealed release over the private data plane.

A final commitment concludes candidate selection for that replicate. The identical frozen strategy and trained artifact, when present, are employed for sealed assessment. Recovery resumes the recorded operation and accepted progress, without authorizing a new candidate or generating additional paid model responses. Any separate replay or modified evaluation contract produces an independent result, thereby preserving the original commitment.

Although this boundary delineates runtime access and evaluation ownership, it does not ensure that historical information was excluded from a model's training data or a participant's prior resources. Organizers must therefore document cohort construction, prior data publication, exploratory experiments, and the method freeze point. Furthermore, because public leaderboard feedback can iteratively influence subsequent submissions, formal campaign registration specifies the intended experimental scope and disclosure policy, recording all attempts considered for the reported comparison. Periodically refreshed or separately sequestered final evaluation windows represent prospective governance extensions rather than intrinsic properties of the current runtime environment.

\subsection{Diversity Construction}
\label{app:diversity}
% DRAFT (Claude): parameters from the v9 suite; prose to be polished by Gemini.

A strategy declares between 0 and 32 features, each with a unique name and a direction of $+1$ or $-1$, and returns their daily values alongside its scores. Diversity counts only features that are \emph{qualified}, \emph{used}, and \emph{mutually independent}. A feature is qualified when its mean daily RankIC, signed by its declared direction, is above zero. It is used when its daily ranks explain part of the strategy's daily score ranks under a score-rank regression, with a unique contribution of at least $0.01$; declaring an unused feature earns nothing. For two features $f$ and $g$, similarity $\sigma(f,g)$ is the mean absolute daily rank correlation over dates with sufficient paired observations,
\begin{equation}
    \sigma(f,g)=\frac{1}{|\mathcal{T}_{fg}|}
       \sum_{t\in\mathcal{T}_{fg}}
       \left|\operatorname{Corr}\!\left(
          \operatorname{rank}(f_t),\operatorname{rank}(g_t)\right)\right|,
    \label{eq:feature_similarity}
\end{equation}
computed only when their overlap coverage reaches $0.9$. Qualified, used features form a graph with an edge whenever $\sigma(f,g)\ge0.8$, and $K$ is the number of connected components. The diversity component is $D=Q\min(K,32)/32$ (Equation~\eqref{eq:efficiency}). No reference factor bank or novelty term enters $D$. Graph components are correlation-based groups and carry no claim of statistical independence or distinct economic mechanism. Unverified feature use leaves $D$ unavailable rather than zero.

\subsection{Metric Availability and Ranking Eligibility}

Execution completion, strategy freezing, metric availability, and leaderboard eligibility constitute distinct operational states. Consequently, a finite research return can coexist with an insufficient sample size for information coefficient (IC) estimation, an unavailable full-market benchmark, or unverified feature utilization. Each metric maintains either its evaluated magnitude or an explicit justification for its unavailability, alongside its associated sample size and coverage. The absence of supplementary evidence does not invalidate completed financial performance.

Financial rankings are evaluated strictly under their registered financial, resource, and risk specifications, without requiring unrelated IC or diversity evidence. Conversely, the composite metric in Equation~\eqref{eq:overall} additionally mandates the verification of its nonzero-weight components, calibration, and comprehensive usage evidence. In particular, a default positive diversity weight renders unverified feature use incompatible with a complete composite, although standalone financial results remain fully reportable. These qualification rules are established prior to observing final outcomes.

\paragraph{Verification evidence.}
Contract tests verify deterministic temporal alignment, the rejection of unauthorized observations, budget accounting, final-commitment rules, and recovery behavior. Empirical consistency between research pipelines and Arena entry points further demands identical factor definitions, evaluation dates, market releases, parameters, runtime environments, and comprehensive financial records. Neither a shared evaluation framework nor passing synthetic test suites alone suffice to guarantee deployed parity. Crucially, characterizing operational throughput and runtime overhead requires empirical measurements gathered from full campaign executions.
