\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:exp_setup}

All experiments use suite \texttt{sandbox\_2022\_2023\_research\_2024\_final\_v9} (Table~\ref{tab:daily_protocol}). The research (in-sample) window is 2022--2023 and the sealed final (out-of-sample) window is calendar 2024; no cross-year experiment is run. Each run has a total budget of 500{,}000 model tokens and at most five research evaluations. No separate per-call output limit or platform-imposed reasoning effort is set. Models may read platform documents on request; document reads are ordinary model calls and count against the same budget. Research feedback is monthly, and strategy failures are reported with error type and location.

\paragraph{Harnesses.}
\emph{One-shot} returns one complete strategy without research feedback or model-assisted repair; the platform then replays it on the research window without returning the result to the model or charging the budget. \emph{Vanilla} is a model--feedback conversation: the model may use permitted research feedback over up to five evaluations and freezes one completed trial. Platform failures and cancellations do not consume evaluations.

\paragraph{Replicates.}
E1 crosses the six models with the two harnesses and runs three independent replicates per cell, for \EOnePrescribed\ prescribed runs. Replicates share no conversation, candidate, or final result. In each cell the first run was designated as the leaderboard entry before any result was observed; the remaining replicates are used only for this paper. Failed runs stay in the registry and in every denominator; a replacement run is added alongside a failed one rather than substituted for it.

\paragraph{Reference line.}
Navers is a fixed reference strategy evaluated once per window with the same replay engine and the platform's scoring. It was not selected using 2024 data. It makes no model calls, so token efficiency is not applicable, and it is scored with the system-track weights ($S=80Q+20D$).

\paragraph{Observed failures.}
One Claude Opus 5.5 one-shot run failed with a protocol violation (\texttt{INVALID\_PROPOSAL}: the replicate did not complete the required protocol) after one model call of 3{,}653 tokens. It is kept in the registry, and a replacement run was submitted for the same cell. At the snapshot, three further runs were blocked: two Opus runs on an unresolved model call (\texttt{MODEL\_CALL\_UNCERTAIN}), which the platform holds for reconciliation instead of issuing a new paid call, and one Astra one-shot run on an unavailable dependency. Both completed Astra one-shot runs finished their sealed final evaluation, but the platform's research-window replay failed for them (\texttt{RESEARCH\_EVALUATION\_FAILED}), so $S_{\mathrm{in}}$ and hence the total score are unavailable; their 2024 financial metrics appear in Table~\ref{tab:e1_main}.

\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
% DRAFT (Claude): to be polished by Gemini.

\paragraph{Temporal contamination.} The sealed window is calendar 2024, which likely precedes the training cutoffs of all evaluated models. Runtime authorization prevents strategies from reading 2024 data during research, but it cannot rule out that a model memorized 2024 market behavior during pretraining. No contamination audit was run for this version, so sealed-window results should be read as historical out-of-sample performance rather than as forward-looking evidence. A forward-looking final window that opens after strategies are frozen is the planned remedy.

\paragraph{Relay endpoints.} All model calls in this paper pass through relay endpoints rather than the providers' official APIs. We therefore cannot verify that a relay served the stated model revision, and token counts are those reported through the relay.

\paragraph{Scale.} Three replicates per cell describe run-to-run variability but do not establish statistical significance, and repeated runs on one market window measure model randomness rather than generalization across markets or periods. The universe covers Shenzhen A-shares only.

\paragraph{Scoring.} The out-of-sample combination is not disclosed and may change; the published in-sample formula and weights were chosen by the organizers, and weight sensitivity is not yet analyzed.
