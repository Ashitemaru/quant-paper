\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:exp_setup}

All experiments are conducted using the \texttt{sandbox\_2022\_2023\_research\_2024\_final\_v9} suite (Table~\ref{tab:daily_protocol}). The research (in-sample) window spans 2022--2023, while the sealed final (out-of-sample) window corresponds to calendar year 2024; no cross-year experiments are executed. Each run is allocated a cumulative budget of 500{,}000 model tokens and a maximum of five research evaluations. No independent per-call output limit or platform-imposed reasoning effort constraint is applied. Models may access platform documentation on demand; such requests are treated as standard model calls and accrue against the shared budget. Research feedback is provided at a monthly cadence, and any strategy execution failures are reported along with their error type and location.

\paragraph{Harnesses.}
\emph{One-shot} generates one complete strategy without research feedback or model-assisted repair; the platform then replays it on the research window without returning results to the model or charging the budget. \emph{Vanilla} implements an interactive model--feedback dialogue in which the model may leverage permitted research feedback across up to five evaluations before freezing one completed trial. Platform failures and cancellations do not consume evaluations.

\paragraph{Replicates.}
E1 crosses the six models with the two harnesses and executes three independent replicates per cell, yielding \EOnePrescribed\ prescribed runs. Replicates share no conversation history, candidates, or final results. Within each cell, the first run was designated as the leaderboard entry prior to observing any outcomes; the remaining replicates are analyzed solely for this paper. Failed runs remain in the registry and in every denominator; a replacement run is appended alongside a failed attempt rather than substituted for it.

\paragraph{Reference line.}
Navers serves as a fixed reference strategy evaluated once per window using the identical replay engine and platform scoring framework. It was not selected using 2024 data. As it requires no model calls, token efficiency is not applicable, and it is scored using the system-track weights ($S=80Q+20D$).

\paragraph{Observed failures.}
One Claude Opus 5.5 one-shot run failed due to a protocol violation (\texttt{INVALID\_PROPOSAL}: the replicate did not complete the required protocol) after one model call of 3{,}653 tokens. This run is retained in the registry, and a replacement run was submitted for the same cell. At the time of the snapshot, three further runs were blocked: two Opus runs stalled on an unresolved model call (\texttt{MODEL\_CALL\_UNCERTAIN}), which the platform holds for reconciliation rather than issuing a new paid call, and one Astra one-shot run was blocked by an unavailable dependency. Although both completed Astra one-shot runs finished their sealed final evaluation, the platform's research-window replay failed (\texttt{RESEARCH\_EVALUATION\_FAILED}), rendering $S_{\mathrm{in}}$ and the overall score unavailable; their 2024 financial metrics are presented in Table~\ref{tab:e1_main}.

\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
% DRAFT (Claude): to be polished by Gemini.

\paragraph{Temporal contamination.} The sealed window spans calendar 2024, which likely precedes the pretraining data horizons of all evaluated models. Although runtime authorization prevents strategies from accessing 2024 data during development, it cannot eliminate the possibility that an underlying model memorized 2024 market dynamics during pretraining. Because a formal contamination audit was not conducted for this version, sealed-window results should be interpreted as historical out-of-sample performance rather than definitive forward-looking evidence. A prospective evaluation window initiated strictly after strategies are frozen represents the planned remedy.

\paragraph{Relay endpoints.} All model queries in this paper are routed through relay endpoints rather than direct provider APIs. Consequently, we cannot independently verify that a relay served the stated model revision, and token metrics reflect the counts reported directly by the relay.

\paragraph{Scale.} While three replicates per cell capture run-to-run variability, they do not establish formal statistical significance. Furthermore, repeated evaluations on a single market window reflect intrinsic model stochasticity rather than generalization across distinct markets or temporal periods. In addition, the asset universe is restricted exclusively to Shenzhen A-shares.

\paragraph{Scoring.} The out-of-sample aggregation scheme remains undisclosed and subject to change. The published in-sample formula and corresponding weights were established by the organizers, and a formal sensitivity analysis of these weights has not yet been conducted.
