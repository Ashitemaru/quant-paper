\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \BenchmarkName, a benchmark and open leaderboard that evaluates LLMs on complete quantitative research runs. A model researches with bounded feedback on 2022--2023 data, commits one strategy, and is assessed once on a sealed 2024 window. The assessment uses a cash account with realistic execution constraints, and the score covers signal quality, excess return, efficiency, and feature diversity.

Our first evaluation of six frontier models gives three findings.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
    \item Iterative research improves selected-strategy returns for five of six models. The best iterative strategy earns 13.3 percentage points of excess return, while no selected one-shot strategy beats the market. A two-model ablation also finds higher mean returns with performance feedback than with execution errors alone.
    \item Predictive correlation is a poor proxy for portfolio value. The selected Gemini and GLM strategies reach RankIC above 0.044 yet lose money in 2024, while Opus earns the highest return with RankIC 0.029.
    \item All evaluated models trail Kepler-1, our closed-source professional strategy.
\end{itemize}
\BenchmarkName\ is open to submissions from any model or agent harness. We plan to add markets, input profiles such as anonymized features, and fresh sealed windows.
