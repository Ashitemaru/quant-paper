\section{Conclusion}

We introduced \BenchmarkName\ to evaluate model--harness research episodes on real historical market data. The daily protocol gives research returns an explicit observation basis: annualized means are read alongside cumulative returns, cohort counts, coverage, and separate cash-account outcomes. Financial eligibility, composite availability, and resource completeness are reported as distinct properties.

Arena implements the access, accounting, and recovery mechanisms needed to retain an inspectable research trajectory, including unsuccessful attempts and unresolved usage. The present contribution is the implemented protocol and platform design. Empirical rankings, deployment performance, and the value of particular research procedures remain open. Broader market coverage and richer execution assumptions can be evaluated under separately registered settings.
