\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \BenchmarkName, a benchmark and open leaderboard that evaluates LLMs on complete quantitative research runs. A model researches with bounded feedback on 2022--2023 data, commits one strategy, and is assessed once on a sealed 2024 window. The assessment uses a cash account with realistic execution constraints, and the score emphasizes realized excess return while also covering signal quality, efficiency, and feature diversity.

Our first evaluation of six frontier models gives four findings.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
    \item Iterative research raises the overall score over one-shot generation for five of six models, and a two-model ablation finds higher mean returns with performance feedback than with execution errors alone.
    \item Out-of-sample profitability is rare. Only two of 27 completed runs beat the equal-weight market in 2024, and none of the strategies reported in Table~\ref{tab:main} does.
    \item Predictive correlation is a poor proxy for portfolio value. GLM and Gemini, the iterative strategies with the highest RankIC, lose money in 2024, while Kimi matches Astra's $9.8\%$ ARR with a RankIC of only 0.007.
    \item All evaluated models trail Kepler-1, our closed-source professional strategy, by more than 20 score points.
\end{itemize}
\BenchmarkName\ is open to submissions from any model or agent harness. We plan to add markets, input profiles such as anonymized features, and fresh sealed windows.
