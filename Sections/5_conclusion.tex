\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \BenchmarkName, a released benchmark for autonomous quantitative research on real historical market data. It makes the complete research run the unit of comparison, combining controlled feedback, strategy commitment, sealed final assessment, and distinct predictive, financial, and execution evidence.

The updated pilot includes 24 completed main-experiment strategies and four completed feedback controls. Offline cash-account evaluation finds positive returns in eight main-experiment runs, but positive excess over the gross market reference in only two. Eight of nine completed iterative runs improve their research-window excess metric. Intermediate candidates lack final-window assessments, and higher RankIC coexists with lower account returns in the Gemini comparison. These findings leave the generalization benefit of candidate revision unresolved. Frozen-candidate replays and controlled feedback comparisons are needed to determine when research progress generalizes.

Public materials describe evaluation dimensions and the research-window scoring rule. Final-score construction and exact execution timing remain private. Offline revised results are distinguished from historical official scorecards and do not establish an updated online leaderboard.
