\section{Conclusion}

We introduced \BenchmarkName, a benchmark and execution platform for evaluating complete, budgeted quantitative research runs on real historical market data. Its participant is a language model together with a research harness; its evaluation contract fixes the inputs, temporal permissions, feedback, resources, final commitment, and financial measurement. The daily protocol makes signal alignment, tied selection, missing observations, research returns, and score qualification explicit, while preserving separate evidence for cash-account behavior.

The Arena platform operationalizes this contract through controlled data access, isolated quantitative execution, durable resource accounting, and recoverable progress. Together, these mechanisms provide a common basis for comparing how research agents use limited resources and respond to empirical feedback. The reserved leaderboard and RSI Harness sections will add campaign findings and a concrete research procedure under this contract. Broader market coverage and richer execution models can be introduced as separately registered benchmark settings.
