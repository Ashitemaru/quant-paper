\abstract{%
Quantitative research is an iterative loop: a researcher proposes a market hypothesis, writes an executable signal, tests it on historical data, and decides what to keep. Existing benchmarks score formulas, code, or individual trading decisions, so they cannot tell whether large language models (LLMs) can run this loop end to end and produce strategies that still work after it closes. We introduce \textbf{\BenchmarkName}, a benchmark and public leaderboard in which a model conducts a complete research run on real Shenzhen A-share data. The model researches with feedback restricted to 2022--2023, freezes one strategy, and is assessed once on a sealed 2024 window that it never observes. Strategies are executed on a cash account with lot sizes and price-limit constraints. They are scored on signal quality, excess return over the market, token efficiency, and the diversity of the features they actually use. We evaluate six frontier models under two tracks, one-shot generation and iterative research. Iterative research with feedback improves every model over one-shot generation. No one-shot strategy beats the equal-weight market in 2024, while the best iterative run (\ModelClaudeOpus) earns 13.3 percentage points of excess return. Yet all models remain far below a professional reference strategy, which scores 51.6 against the best model's 31.9. A higher predictive correlation also often fails to translate into a better portfolio. \BenchmarkName\ is open for submissions with any model or agent harness.
}
