\abstract{%
Quantitative research is an iterative loop: a researcher proposes a market hypothesis, implements it as an executable signal, tests it on historical data, and decides what to keep. Existing benchmarks score formulas, code, or individual trading decisions, leaving open whether large language models (LLMs) can complete this loop and produce strategies that work beyond the research period. We introduce \textbf{\BenchmarkName}, a benchmark and public leaderboard for end-to-end quantitative research on real A-share market data. A model constructs features and stock-scoring code, receives feedback only on 2022--2023, and freezes one strategy for assessment on the sealed year 2024. A cash-account evaluator applies trading constraints and costs, including market impact estimated from a simulated order book. Scores are dominated by realized excess return and complemented by signal quality and the diversity of features used by the strategy; token usage is reported but not scored. We evaluate six frontier models in one-shot and iterative settings. Iterative research raises the overall score for five of the six models, and \ModelAstra\ leads with the lowest token cost among iterative runs. In a two-model feedback ablation, withholding performance metrics while retaining execution errors lowers mean 2024 returns for both models. Yet beating the market remains hard: only two of 27 completed runs earn positive 2024 excess return, strategies with the highest predictive correlation are not the most profitable, and all models trail Kepler-1, our closed-source professional strategy, by more than 30 score points. \BenchmarkName\ is open to submissions from any model or agent harness.
}
