\section{Campaign Records and Reproducibility}
\label{app:registry}

The empirical results use the 20:27 \texttt{raw-results-20261018} export: one JSONL record and one CSV row per episode. All 106 identifiers and statuses agree between the files; 2,712 scalar fields were reconciled. Historical summaries are generated from the JSONL scorecards, with the CSV serving as a cross-check. E1 and E2 are grouped separately by suite identity. Main financial results additionally use the supplied offline account records: 208 statistics were verified directly against saved NAV series. Each episode has one required replicate. Cross-episode summaries use sample standard deviations, without interpreting the within-episode, single-replicate standard deviation of zero as experimental stability.

The public suite configuration and exported scorecards establish the reported population, resource limits, statuses, and metric values. The historical results and the revised public research protocol have distinct version provenance. Public replication covers aggregation of reported outcomes; execution schedules, data cutoffs, and final-score construction remain private. Hosted re-evaluation requires the organizer's registered evaluator.

\paragraph{Result retention and selection.}
The aggregation retains all provided episodes. Cancellations are a separate category, not silently removed observations. Completed episodes with a missing research-window score remain in financial and resource summaries. Ongoing submissions are retained at their exported state rather than replaced by later live results. The handoff report's intended three runs per model--track cell is not a complete preregistration manifest, so observed submission counts are not relabelled as prescribed replicates.

\paragraph{Coverage and uncertainty.}
The historical scorecards provide coverage buckets and aggregate financial metrics; their within-window confidence fields are null. The updated archive also contains daily account series for temporal diagnostics. We report cross-run dispersion where available; repeated runs on one market interval do not supply independent market samples. Exact observation schedules and trading records are not disclosed, and statistical significance is not inferred from the aggregate metrics.

\paragraph{Financial semantics and disclosure.}
The historical tables in this appendix use additive research returns, paired excess ARR, and additive drawdown. Main tables explicitly use offline cash-account statistics and versioned offline totals. Neither set establishes deployment of the revised scorer. The public protocol describes evaluation dimensions and the revised research formula. Final-score transformations, weights, aggregation, exact execution times, and data-availability cutoffs are outside the public disclosure scope.
